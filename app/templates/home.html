One-time by default

The first open is the only open. After that the link answers 410 Gone, permanently — including for a second click from the same person.

③

Budgeted uses

Send it to three people and allow three opens. The counter is incremented in one database transaction, so simultaneous clicks cannot exceed the budget.

↗

You see the chain

Every open logs time, device, referrer and IP. When a link turns up somewhere you did not send it, you get an alert instead of finding out later.

What “shared” means here

A short link is anonymous by design — a recipient has no name until they identify themselves. We record three honest signals instead of inventing one: a second device opening the same link, a referrer from a mail or messaging host, and a pass-along link a recipient creates with the Share button. Each of those is recorded and can raise an alert.
