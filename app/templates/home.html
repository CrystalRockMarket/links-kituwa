A link that knows when it has been used — and stops working when it should.

Paste any URL. Get a short link back. Set how many times it may be opened, from once to unlimited. Keep a private log of every open, every forward and every device that received it.

Create an account Sign in

One-time by default

The first open is the only open. After that the link answers 410 Gone, permanently. Send it to two friends and only one gets through.

Budgeted uses

Need it for three people? Set three. The counter is incremented inside a single database transaction, so simultaneous clicks cannot exceed the budget.

You see the chain

Every open logs time, device, referrer and IP. When a link turns up somewhere you did not send it, you get an alert instead of finding out later.

What "shared" means here

A short link is anonymous by design — we cannot know a recipient's name. We can see when the same link is opened by a second device, from a mail or messaging referrer, or through a pass-along link one of your recipients created with the Share button. Each of those is recorded and can raise an alert.
