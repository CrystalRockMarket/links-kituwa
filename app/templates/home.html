Zero-knowledge

AES-256-GCM with a fresh 256-bit key per link, generated by your browser. The key never leaves the fragment, so it is never sent to the server.

No tracking

No IP addresses, user agents, referrers or device fingerprints. There is no per-click log to leak, subpoena or sell.

Gone means gone

When the uses run out or the time passes, the ciphertext is overwritten in the database. A preview fetch never spends a use; only a person clicking does.
